AI pricing models and consumption-based billing have created unpredictable software costs that challenge traditional enterprise budget forecasting. According to Zylo's 2026 SaaS Management Index, 78% of IT leaders experienced unexpected AI or consumption-related charges in the past year. Organizations are urged to adopt continuous governance and cross-functional operational discipline to manage software spend as a strategic capability rather than a periodic cost optimization exercise.
