Model Context Protocol is an open standard enabling AI models to securely connect with enterprise SaaS applications, APIs, and databases. The article details how MCP works through a client-server architecture, explores common use cases including AI copilots and workflow automation, and discusses governance challenges such as shadow AI and permission management that enterprises must address as AI adoption scales across fragmented technology environments.
